# LegalMind — User Guide

## 1. Purpose

LegalMind assists reviewers with structured analysis of commercial and
contract documents.

It is a legal intelligence and research-support system, not a replacement
for qualified legal counsel.

## 2. Document Analysis

1. Open the LegalMind dashboard.
2. Select a supported document.
3. Upload the document.
4. Start document analysis.
5. Review extracted clauses and identified risks.
6. Review supporting research evidence.
7. Review reasoning and recommendations.
8. Review redlines and explainability information.
9. Resolve HITL actions when analysis is paused.

## 3. Supported Upload Types

The application upload endpoint supports:

- PDF
- DOCX
- JPG
- JPEG
- PNG

The configured upload limit is 10 MB.

## 4. Human Review

When the risk threshold requires human review, the reviewer can:

- APPROVE
- REJECT
- EDIT

Reviewer actions are recorded in the audit trail.

## 5. Reports

The application generates a DOCX report containing the available analysis,
risk information, evidence, recommendations, and legal disclaimer.

## 6. Jurisdiction

The current Pakistan deployment uses the validated statutory corpora:

- Contract Act 1872
- Companies Act 2017
- Companies Regulations 2024

Pakistan case-law retrieval is not currently configured.

## 7. Important Notice

All LegalMind results must be reviewed by a qualified legal professional
before reliance or action.
